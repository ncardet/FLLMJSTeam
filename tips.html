💡 Team Know-How

Tips & Tricks

Strategy that works for any season, plus mission-by-mission advice for BIOGLOW. Master these and watch the field light up.

🗺️ The Big Picture

General Strategy

🎯

Plan Your Run Route

  • Map out which missions your robot will attempt before every practice run — don't just wing it.
  • Group nearby missions into a single trip to reduce travel time.
  • Identify your "anchor" missions (easy, reliable points) and always do those first.
  • Use the Score Calculator to figure out the highest-value combination of missions.
  • Have a Plan A and a Plan B in case something goes wrong mid-run.
💡 The team that scores the most doesn't always attempt the most missions — they attempt the right missions consistently.
🔋

Protect Your Precision Tokens

  • Precision Tokens are worth up to 50 points — more than any single mission!
  • Every interruption outside home costs a token. Only touch the robot when it's back in home.
  • If your robot gets stuck mid-run, wait a moment to see if it recovers before grabbing it.
  • Practice until your robot rarely needs to be interrupted.
⚠️ The first interruption is free (still 50), but the second drops you to 35 and it keeps falling: 25, 15, 10, 0.
⏱️

Time Management

  • You have exactly 150 seconds. Time every practice run with a stopwatch.
  • Know the time each mission segment takes — cut ones that consistently eat up too much time.
  • Factor in travel time between missions — it adds up fast.
  • Aim to finish with 10-20 seconds to spare so you're not rushing the last mission.
  • Simpler missions at the end of the run are safer when you're short on time.
💡 A robot that completes 6 missions perfectly scores more than one that attempts 12 and fails half.
🔄

Consistency is King

  • Aim for the same score 10 runs in a row, not one perfect run.
  • A mission that succeeds 8 out of 10 times is more valuable than one that succeeds 3 out of 10.
  • Log your scores from every practice run to track improvement over time.
  • When something breaks, fix it — don't band-aid over problems.
📋

Pre-Match Checklist

  • Verify your robot, attachments, and keystone species all fit completely in one launch area.
  • Check that robot height is under 12 inches (305 mm).
  • Charge your battery fully — bring a spare if allowed.
  • Test your first two missions in the practice area before your match.
  • Have one team member assigned to watch the clock and call out time warnings.
  • Double-check all attachment connections — things come loose in transit!
⚠️ Equipment Inspection is 20 free points — and this year your keystone species model (M13) has to fit too!
🤝

Teamwork & Roles

  • Assign clear roles: Driver (touches the robot), Navigator (calls out the plan), Timer.
  • The driver should stay focused on the field — the navigator communicates with referees and tracks time.
  • Practice transitions between your team's sub-runs so there's no hesitation.
  • Celebrate small wins during practice — positivity builds better teams.

🤖 Build Smarter

Robot Design Tips

🏗️

Base Robot Design

  • Build a compact, low base — staying under 12 inches is non-negotiable for Equipment Inspection.
  • Use a sturdy, symmetrical drive base. Wobbly robots drift off course.
  • Keep your center of gravity low and centered to prevent tipping.
  • Use the same wheel type on both sides and ensure they're balanced.
  • Protect motors and the hub from accidental bumps to mission elements.
🔧

Attachments & Tools

  • Design attachments that are quick to swap — use simple push-on connectors.
  • One good attachment that handles multiple missions beats five fragile single-purpose ones.
  • Test each attachment at least 20 times before competition.
  • Color-code attachments so the team can swap them fast without confusion.
  • Always reinforce attachment connection points — they're the most common failure spot.
  • Add a short back-away move after every No Equipment Constraint mission so nothing is still touching the model at the end.
💡 The fewer attachments you need to swap mid-run, the more time you save.
📐

Navigation & Alignment

  • Use wall-following techniques at the start of each sub-run for consistent alignment.
  • Add physical guides or "jig" pieces to help the robot align precisely with mission elements.
  • Mark your launch position on the robot and the field — consistency starts at home.
  • Gyro sensors are powerful for straight-line movement — calibrate before every match.
  • Slow down before critical moments — speed is useless if the robot overshoots.
🔌

SPIKE Prime Best Practices

  • Always start programs with the robot battery above 80% charge.
  • Calibrate the gyro sensor at room temperature — it can drift if it gets warm.
  • Use wait_for_seconds carefully — it can cause timing issues. Prefer encoder-based movement.
  • Test programs after any hardware changes — small builds can alter motor performance.
  • Save backup versions of programs before making large changes.

🌿 Mission by Mission

Mission-Specific Tips

Targeted advice for all 15 BIOGLOW missions, Equipment Inspection, and Precision Tokens.

🔍

Equipment Inspection

  • Measure height with every attachment and the keystone species loaded — before inspection, not after.
  • Keep a 12-inch ruler and a launch-area template at your team table.
  • If you’re close to the limit, build shorter — referees measure from the mat.
💡 Value: 20 pts — always achievable. Never miss this one.
🛸

M01 · Drone Survey

  • Getting the drone off the mat is the easier 20 points — start there.
  • The LiDAR map flip takes more control. Test it separately before combining.
  • No Equipment Constraint: make sure the robot backs away from the model before time runs out.
💡 Value: up to 30 pts.
💥

M02 · Exploding Seeds

  • Each seed off the stalk is 10 pts — three seeds total.
  • Think ahead: these seeds can score again in M14. A basket or fence on the robot can catch them.
  • Test where the seeds land when released so you know how to collect them.
💡 Value: up to 30 pts here, plus more in M14.
🪨

M03 · Flip the Rock

  • The research flag is the easier 20 points.
  • For the +10 bonus, the rock has to end back in its original starting position — practice a gentle return.
  • Mark the rock’s starting spot on your practice field so you can check it fast.
💡 Value: up to 30 pts.
🦗

M04 · Lucky Leaves

  • The katydid’s starting position changes, so don’t build for only one setup — practice them all.
  • If the katydid ends the match outside its habitat, the whole mission is 0. Slow and steady!
  • If it ever leaves the habitat completely during the match, you lose the bonus even if it comes back.
💡 Value: up to 30 pts — one leaf is 10, the second leaf bonus is 20.
⚠️ No Equipment Constraint — back away from the nest before the end.
🌱

M05 · Reaching Roots

  • Partly extended is 10, fully extended is 20 — aim for full.
  • A fully extended root also helps the team on the other table earn their M07 bonus. That’s Coopertition!
  • No Equipment Constraint — don’t leave an attachment resting on the root.
💡 Value: up to 20 pts (and it helps another team!).
🐜

M06 · Leafcutter Frenzy

  • The ant must be touching the nest or no fragments count.
  • Each fragment inside the nest is 10 pts, up to 4.
  • This mission rewards control more than speed — move slowly so the fragments don’t scatter.
💡 Value: up to 40 pts — one of the biggest missions.
🍄

M07 · Humongous Fungus

  • Fully extending the mycelium is 20 pts.
  • Bonuses come from connections with the other team’s plant root (M05). If both teams fully extend, both teams earn them.
  • Talk to the team on the other table before your match — this is Coopertition in action.
  • Watch that chains or parts don’t catch on your attachment as you pull away.
💡 Value: up to 40 pts with both connection bonuses.
🌿

M08 · Tangled

  • The vine just needs to end up touching the mat — 30 pts, no bonus.
  • A simple, reliable attachment is perfect here. Great early build target!
💡 Value: 30 pts. Great points for an easy mechanism.
📷

M09 · Research Platform

  • Three separate 10-pt parts: raise the platform, deploy the camera trap, free the seed.
  • You can score just one or two if the others aren’t reliable yet.
  • The seed can go on to M14 Seeds of Renewal.
💡 Value: up to 30 pts.
⚠️ No Equipment Constraint — back away before the end.
🕷️

M10 · Fragile Microhabitats

  • These 20 points start out yours — you keep them by NOT bumping the spider or snail habitats.
  • Plan every route around these models. Drive slowly near them.
  • Check them after every practice run to see if a route is too close.
💡 Value: 20 pts for being careful.
🪟

M11 · Window to the Past

  • Get the root cover down so it’s touching the mat.
  • There’s no equipment restriction here, so it can be a good mission to finish a run on.
💡 Value: 20 pts.
🌳

M12 · Forest Elder

  • Raising the cane so it touches the tree is the easier 20.
  • The support tie around the post is 10 more — treat it as extra once the cane is reliable.
💡 Value: up to 30 pts.
⚠️ No Equipment Constraint — back away before the end.
🦜

M13 · Keystone Species

  • Design your own keystone species from the bricks in bag 20 — research a real rainforest keystone species for ideas!
  • It counts as equipment, so it has to fit in your launch area during inspection. Build it early and test the fit.
  • It scores when it’s on the restoration platform and the young trees are raised.
💡 Value: 30 pts — and a great story for the judges.
🌰

M14 · Seeds of Renewal

  • Each seed in the replantation station is 5 pts, and 5 more if it’s also touching the mat (up to 4 seeds).
  • Seeds come from M02 and M09, so plan those missions and M14 together.
  • Try a basket with a bottom that opens when it hits the model, so seeds drop all the way down.
💡 Value: up to 40 pts if you collect all 4 seeds.
🏡

M15 · Biocentric Architecture

  • Three upgrades: nesting canopy (10), garden skylight (10), compost hatch (10).
  • The +10 Environmental Bonus depends on the dock: Mine → nesting canopy, City → garden skylight, Farm → compost hatch.
  • Test all three docks and choose the one where your robot is most reliable.
💡 Value: up to 40 pts.
⚠️ No Equipment Constraint — back away before the end.
🎯

Precision Tokens

  • Worth up to 50 pts just for not interrupting the robot outside home.
  • Design fully autonomous programs that return to home on their own.
  • Remember: 6 or 5 tokens left both score 50, so one interruption isn’t a disaster — two starts to hurt.
⚠️ This shapes your whole strategy. Plan every run around keeping your tokens!

💡 Beyond the Robot

Innovation Project Tips

Our challenge: find a problem that puts biodiversity at risk and design a solution that could help.

🔎

Pick a Real Problem

  • Look for a specific threat to biodiversity — habitat loss, invasive species, pollution, or something in our own neighborhood.
  • The more specific the problem, the stronger the solution.
  • Use the Engineering Notebook pages to record your research.
💡 Judges love when you can explain exactly who or what the problem affects.
🧪

Design & Test a Solution

  • Brainstorm lots of ideas before picking one.
  • Build a model, drawing, or prototype — anything that shows how it works.
  • Test it or get feedback, then improve it. Write down every change.
📣

Share It

  • Talk to an expert — a scientist, park ranger, or zoo educator — and record what they said.
  • Share your idea with an audience beyond your team (another class, family, community).
  • Tell the judges how the feedback changed your project.

🏆 Event Day

Event Day Wisdom

😌

Stay Calm & Trust Your Practice

  • Nerves are normal — take a deep breath before each match.
  • Trust your robot. You’ve practiced this. Stick to the plan.
  • Don’t change your strategy at the last minute unless you have a very good reason.
  • Only your best of three matches counts. A rough Match 1 is just information for Match 2.
🔬

Use Practice Time Wisely

  • Use the practice field to confirm your robot runs as expected — not to try new things.
  • Check that the field models are set up like yours (referees set them up).
  • Ask the team on the other table about M05/M07 so you can both earn the connection bonus.
🦋

The Keystone Mindset

  • In a rainforest, every species helps the others survive. On our team, every person matters too.
  • If something goes wrong, adapt and keep going.
  • Gracious Professionalism is scored at the robot table — cheer for other teams and thank the volunteers.
  • Win or lose, celebrate what we built and learned together.
💡 A healthy ecosystem is a team effort. So is a great robot.
