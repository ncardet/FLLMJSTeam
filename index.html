🦋 BIOGLOW at a Glance Founders Edition · Challenge

This season takes us into the rainforest to explore biodiversity and habitat recovery. Our robot will complete missions modeled on real ecological processes — seed dispersal, fungal networks, canopy research, habitat protection, and species conservation. Our Innovation Project asks us to find a problem that puts biodiversity at risk and design a solution that could help.

🗺️ Setting Up the BIOGLOW Field Setup Guide

Before every practice run, the field needs to be set up exactly the same way. Consistent setup means consistent results — small differences in model placement can throw off an entire program!

1Watch the official Field Set-Up video below and keep the Field Set-Up Reference Guide open while you work.
2Lay the mat flat on the table, pushed against the south wall and centered side to side — no bumps or wrinkles.
3Place every mission model exactly as shown. Watch for models with a random starting position (like the katydid in M04) — practice from all of them.
4Decide which dock (Mine, City, or Farm) each dock mission (M13, M14, M15) goes on. Your choice changes the M15 Environmental Bonus — write it down so setup is the same every time.
5Check the launch area: robot, attachments, and your keystone species model must all fit in one launch area under 12 in. (305 mm).
6Have a second person verify the setup before a timed run.
⚠️ Important: At events, referees set up the field. In practice, always reset to the official starting positions between runs — otherwise your practice scores won't match competition conditions.

🎤 Judging Session Innovation + Robot Design

At each event we meet with judges to present our Innovation Project and explain our Robot Design. Judges use the rubrics to score us, and they watch for Core Values the whole time. The flowchart shows the order of the session.

💡 Use the Presentation Builder to work through your Robot Design story one question at a time. It saves as you go, and you can export your notes into slides.
1Read the rubrics together so everyone knows what "Exceeds" looks like.
2Decide who covers which part — every team member should have a speaking role.
3Practice out loud at least five times before an event.
4Do a full run-through with a mock judge before each event — and again at the Dec 3 Practice Competition before the Regional.

📜 Robot Game Rules & Scoring Official Docs

Know the rules cold — referees enforce them exactly. The Robot Game Rulebook covers every mission, the launch areas, interruptions, and equipment rules. Always check the Challenge Updates — they can change how a mission is scored.

📖 Watch for the No Equipment Constraint symbol: on those missions the model scores nothing if your robot or attachment is still touching it when the match ends. Our Missions page marks every one.
