⛔ No Equipment Constraint: on missions with this tag, a model can’t earn points if your equipment is touching it at the end of the match. Program a small back-away move after you finish those missions!
Dock missions (M13–M15) go on one of the three docks — Mine, City, or Farm. Your team chooses, and the choice affects the M15 bonus.
🔍 Inspection Equipment Inspection

Before the match, the referee checks that your robot and all your equipment fit in a single launch area and are under the height limit.

Robot and all equipment fit completely in one launch area, under 12 in. (305 mm) 20 pts
Max possible20 pts
🛸 Mission 01 Drone Survey
⛔ No Equipment Constraint

Drones fly above the canopy and send back data in real time. Help the pilot launch the drone so it can build a LiDAR map of the rainforest.

Drone is no longer touching the mat 20 pts
Bonus: LiDAR map is completely flipped over, with the scan marker at least partly in the survey area +10 pts
Max possible30 pts
💥 Mission 02 Exploding Seeds
🔗 Feeds M14

Some trees fling their seeds to new ground. Release the seeds from the seed pod.

Each seed no longer touching the stalk (3 seeds) 10 pts ea.
Max possible30 pts

💡 These seeds can be collected and delivered to M14 Seeds of Renewal.

🪨 Mission 03 Flip the Rock

Lots of creatures live just under the surface. Flip the rock to see what is underneath — then leave no trace by putting it back.

The research flag is down 20 pts
Bonus: the rock has been returned to its original starting position +10 pts
Max possible30 pts
🦗 Mission 04 Lucky Leaves
⛔ No Equipment Constraint🎲 Random Start

The katydid hides in plain sight among the leaves. Collect leaves from the nest without disturbing this master of camouflage.

One leaf is completely removed, no longer touching the nest 10 pts
Bonus: the second leaf is also removed, with the katydid in its original starting position +20 pts
If the katydid is ever completely outside the leaf habitat during the match, the bonus is lost Rule
The katydid must end the match completely inside the leaf habitat, or this mission scores 0 Rule
Max possible30 pts
🌱 Mission 05 Reaching Roots
⛔ No Equipment Constraint🤝 Coopertition

Help a plant root grow across the forest boundary to find a partner for symbiosis.

Plant root is partially extended 10 pts
Plant root is completely extended 20 pts
Only one of these can score Rule
Max possible20 pts

💡 A fully extended root also lets the team on the other table earn the M07 connection bonus.

🐜 Mission 06 Leafcutter Frenzy

Leafcutter ants carry leaves home to feed their underground fungus garden. Guide the ant back to the nest — go too fast and the leaf fragments scatter.

Ant is touching the nest, and each leaf fragment is contained within the nest (up to 4) 10 pts ea.
Max possible40 pts

💡 No points for fragments unless the ant is touching the nest.

🍄 Mission 07 Humongous Fungus
⛔ No Equipment Constraint🤝 Coopertition

Mycelium is the huge underground fungal network under the forest floor. Form a new connection between the mycelium and a neighboring tree root.

Mycelium is completely extended 20 pts
Bonus: a connection forms between one team’s extended mycelium and the other team’s fully extended plant root — two bonuses possible +10 pts ea.
The mycelium and root don’t need to touch. If both are fully extended, both teams earn the bonus. Rule
Bonus isn’t possible without an opposing team (e.g., remote events) Rule
Max possible40 pts
🌿 Mission 08 Tangled

Vines have wrapped around a tree. Untangle them so the tree has room and sunlight to grow.

Vine is touching the mat 30 pts
Max possible30 pts
📷 Mission 09 Research Platform
⛔ No Equipment Constraint🔗 Feeds M14

Scientists deploy equipment high in the trees to watch canopy wildlife. Set up the research station.

Research platform is raised 10 pts
Camera trap is deployed 10 pts
Seed is no longer touching the tree 10 pts
Max possible30 pts

💡 This seed can also be delivered to M14 Seeds of Renewal.

🕷️ Mission 10 Fragile Microhabitats
⛔ No Equipment Constraint

Explore protected ecosystems without damaging the homes of the tiny creatures that live there.

Spider habitat is in its original starting position 10 pts
Snail habitat is in its original starting position 10 pts
Max possible20 pts

💡 These start as points — you keep them by NOT bumping the habitats.

🪟 Mission 11 Window to the Past

Open the root window to study and record the rainforest’s history.

Root cover is down, touching the mat 20 pts
Max possible20 pts
🌳 Mission 12 Forest Elder
⛔ No Equipment Constraint

An old tree is bending under its own weight. Help stabilize it.

Cane is completely raised, touching the tree 20 pts
Support tie is around the post 10 pts
Max possible30 pts
🦜 Mission 13 Keystone Species
⚓ Dock Mission

Design and build your own keystone species model, then deliver it to a protected habitat on the restoration platform.

Our keystone species is on the restoration platform, and the young trees are raised 30 pts
Build the species from the bricks in bag 20. It counts as equipment and must pass the pre-match inspection. Rule
Max possible30 pts
🌰 Mission 14 Seeds of Renewal
⚓ Dock Mission

A thriving rainforest needs plant diversity. Plant the seeds your team collected so they can take root.

Each seed contained within the replantation station (up to 4) 5 pts ea.
Bonus: each of those seeds is also touching the mat +5 pts ea.
Max possible40 pts

💡 Seeds come from M02 (3 seeds) and M09 (1 seed).

🏡 Mission 15 Biocentric Architecture
⛔ No Equipment Constraint⚓ Dock Mission

Upgrade a building so it gives wildlife a home, restores the soil, and lets in natural light.

Nesting canopy is raised 10 pts
Garden skylight is completely in 10 pts
Compost hatch is completely opened, touching the mat 10 pts
Environmental Bonus: complete the greatest need for the dock this model is on — Mine → nesting canopy · City → garden skylight · Farm → compost hatch +10 pts
Max possible40 pts

💡 Only one Environmental Bonus is possible, and it depends on which dock you choose.

🎯 Precision Tokens Precision Tokens

You start with six precision tokens. The referee removes one every time you interrupt the robot when it is outside home.

6 tokens left 50 pts
5 tokens left 50 pts
4 tokens left 35 pts
3 tokens left 25 pts
2 tokens left 15 pts
1 token left 10 pts
0 tokens left 0 pts
Max possible50 pts

💡 Your first interruption is “free” (still 50) — every one after that costs points.
